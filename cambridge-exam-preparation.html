Cambridge English Exam Preparation

Prepare confidently for your Cambridge English exam

Prepare for your Cambridge English exam with structured, personalized lessons designed to develop your English and give you the skills and strategies you need to perform confidently on exam day.

My Cambridge exam preparation courses are based around the specific requirements of your chosen exam, from A2 Key and B1 Preliminary to B2 First, C1 Advanced and C2 Proficiency. We work through each part of the exam so that you understand exactly what to expect and know how to approach different question types and tasks.

What We Cover

Reading & Use of English

Develop your ability to understand texts quickly and accurately, identify key information, deal with unfamiliar vocabulary and grammar, and approach the different question types strategically. We also work on techniques such as reading for gist, detail and inference, as well as managing your time effectively.

Writing

Learn how to plan, structure and develop your answers for the different Cambridge writing tasks. We focus on the criteria Cambridge examiners use when assessing writing, including content, communicative achievement, organization and language, so you understand what makes an effective exam response. You’ll receive detailed feedback and opportunities to improve and rewrite your work.

Listening

Build your confidence with the different listening tasks and develop strategies for listening for specific information, main ideas, opinions, attitudes and detail. We practice working effectively with the questions before and during each recording.

Speaking

Develop your fluency, pronunciation, vocabulary and ability to express and justify your ideas and opinions. We practice the different parts of the Speaking test, including individual turns, interaction with your speaking partner and responding to questions, using Cambridge-style tasks and assessment criteria.

Exam Skills & Strategy

Knowing English is only part of being ready for a Cambridge exam. You also need to understand how the exam works. Throughout the course, we work on exam technique, time management, common mistakes and strategies for approaching each task effectively.

We use authentic Cambridge-style practice and mock tests to track your progress, identify areas that need more attention and help you become comfortable working under exam conditions.

All lessons are 60, 90, or 120 minutes (as you prefer), giving us the time and flexibility to focus on what is most important to you.
