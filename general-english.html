General English

Build confidence across every area of English

We will exercise all four language skills — reading, listening, writing, and speaking — as well as grammar and vocabulary, with lessons personalized to suit your learning style and objectives.

We will use a variety of materials, such as books, flashcards, articles, and videos to enhance the learning process. Homework and tests are optional.

Lessons for every level

Lessons are available for any level, from complete beginner to advanced, and can be taught individually or in a group (depending on number of students).

All lessons are 60, 90, or 120 minutes (as you prefer), giving us the time and flexibility to focus on what is most important to you.
