About Me

Hi there! I’m Amanda.

I’m from the United States, and I have over 15 years of combined career experience in business and teaching English.

I also speak Italian and Spanish.

Connecting with people and learning about different cultures is invaluable to me.

In my free time, I enjoy traveling, movement arts, reading, and studying foreign languages. I also love animals, food, adventure, and nature (especially the beach).

Why Choose Me?

  • I am a certified ESL teacher.
  • I have an MBA.
  • I have years of experience in teaching and in business.
  • I know how it feels to study a foreign language because I am a foreign language student, too.
  • I am a cheerleader at heart, and I encourage my students in every lesson.
  • People are interesting to me, and I enjoy getting to know them.

It's All About You!

I can customize lessons specifically to your needs and goals.

I know how to make you feel more comfortable and confident in English

I will help you nail a job interview and get hired.

I can help you prepare and pass an exam.

I know the terminology and the context to teach you Business English to get that job promotion.

But I need something from you too.

You have to want it. If you are truly invested in learning, the process will be swift.

Stay consistent. The longer you commit to being consistent, the quicker your goal will be reached.

Take notes during our lessons. I suggest writing notes by hand because it forces you to process information more deeply than typing.

Be humble. Learning a language requires vulnerability. It can be intimidating to make mistakes, but these are necessary steps to learn the language.

Keep an open mind. Just as in life, there are exceptions to the rules of English.

Serious inquiries only. Are you ready?
